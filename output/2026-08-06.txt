## 1. Top AI News

1. **Google Maps expansion**: Google Maps has added new features, including food ordering and hotel bookings, transforming it from a navigation tool to a real-world task assistant. Why it matters: This shift indicates a significant change in how users interact with Google Maps, potentially increasing user engagement and dependence on the platform.
2. **Omilia's funding**: Omilia raised $67M to scale its customer support platform, achieving a 10x increase in ARR to $60 million. Why it matters: This funding round demonstrates the growing importance of AI-powered customer support, highlighting the need for companies to invest in efficient and effective support solutions.
3. **Meta's Muse Code**: Meta launched Muse Code, an AI agent for handling complex tasks with large code bases. Why it matters: This development showcases Meta's commitment to AI-powered coding solutions, which could revolutionize the way developers work and collaborate.
4. **Klaviyo's acquisition**: Klaviyo acquired Elias Torres' agency, reuniting tech founders and expanding its AI capabilities. Why it matters: This acquisition highlights the growing trend of e-commerce companies investing in AI-powered solutions to enhance customer experiences and streamline operations.
5. **Jeff Dean's departure**: Jeff Dean and other top AI researchers left Google to launch their own startup, focusing on using AI for scientific discovery. Why it matters: This departure signals a significant shift in the AI landscape, as top talent leaves established companies to pursue innovative ventures and push the boundaries of AI research.
